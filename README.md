then please share it with me and the rest of the world. I would love to see your builds!!

NJD V1 DOC.pdf is the complete documentation, it includes schematic, BOM, details of the pcb, wiring diagram,
and a buying list allowing you to directly access a basket at tme.eu.
GERBER NJD V1.zip is the file you need to upload on the website of the pcb manufacturer of your choice to get the pcb built.
ibom.html is an interactive bom, making it easier to build the pcb
you will also find all the kicad files that you can use to make your own mods of the design
